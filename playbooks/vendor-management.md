# Vendor and supplier management

*A practical model for managing providers, resellers and service partners. Tool-neutral.*

The aim is to get the service that was bought, at the price that was agreed, with the risks understood, and to keep the relationship useful for both sides.

## Lifecycle

1. **Need and market check.** Define the requirement first. Ask at least three providers for comparable offers.
2. **Evaluation.** Score on fit, total cost over the term, support model, exit terms and references. Agree the weights before the offers arrive.
3. **Contract.** Cover scope, service levels, response and resolution targets, reporting, price changes, data return and exit.
4. **Onboarding.** Name contacts on both sides and set the escalation path. Give access with least privilege and an end date.
5. **Operation.** Hold regular service reviews and keep SLA reporting, an issue log and a renewal calendar.
6. **Renewal or exit.** Decide with data: performance, cost and alternatives.

## Classify suppliers

| Tier | Description | Management intensity |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Failure stops the business (hosting, connectivity, ERP support) | Monthly review, tested exit plan |
| Important | Failure hurts but has a workaround | Quarterly review |
| Standard | Easily replaced | Annual check |

## Keep one register

For each supplier, record the service, owner, contract dates, renewal notice period, value, service levels, access granted, last review and risks. The register feeds the budget and the audit evidence.

## Good practice

- One internal owner per supplier, so someone is always accountable.
- Third-party access is time-limited, named and logged.
- Check service reports against your own monitoring instead of taking them on trust.
- Negotiate prices on total cost, volume and term, and prepare with a market comparison.
- Keep notes of meetings and decisions. They settle later disagreements.

## Measures

- SLA attainment per supplier.
- Open issues and their age.
- Spend versus contract, and share of spend under contract.
- Number of renewals decided before the notice date.
