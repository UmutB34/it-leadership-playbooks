# Endpoint operations

*Running laptops, desktops and mobile devices across several sites, from purchase to retirement.*

## Goal

Everyone gets a working, secure, current device quickly, wherever they are, and IT always knows what exists and what state it is in.

## Lifecycle

| Stage | What happens | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Procure | Standard models, tracked purchase | Asset record created |
| Enrol | Device joins device management at first start | Enrolment is automatic, not manual |
| Configure | Policies: encryption, screen lock, firewall, update rules | Compliance baseline |
| Deploy apps | Self-service catalogue plus required apps | Packaged and tested installs |
| Maintain | Patch rings, health reports | Patch compliance report |
| Support | Remote first, on-site kit for travel and events | Ticket history per device |
| Retire | Data wipe, record updated, disposal certificate | Evidence kept |

## Device management

- Use one management platform for all supported operating systems. Compliance rules block access from non-compliant devices.
- Identity and device are linked. Access depends on who you are and on whether the device is healthy.
- Mobile devices follow the same enrolment and compliance model for mail and files.

## Operating system and application updates

Updates follow deployment rings (pilot, early, broad). Required applications are pushed, and optional ones come from a self-service catalogue. See patch-management.md.

## Sites abroad and events

- A standard travel kit: pre-configured laptops, secure connectivity, collaboration tools and spare parts.
- A checklist before departure and a hand-back procedure after return.
- A named remote support contact for the whole trip.

## Records

One asset inventory covers hardware, software and licences. I update it at purchase, hand-out, return and disposal. It feeds the budget and audits.

## Measures

- Devices enrolled vs devices owned.
- Patch and compliance rates.
- Time from request to a ready device.
- Support tickets per device and top causes.
- Devices older than the refresh target.

## Why it matters

Standard devices, automatic enrolment and visible compliance cut support cost and shrink the part of the company an attacker can reach.
