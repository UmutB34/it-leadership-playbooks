# Internal audit checklist for IT processes

*What to look at when auditing an IT department's own processes and documents. A checklist of questions, not a record of any organization's findings.*

## Goal

Keep process documents true to how the team really works, and find small gaps before an external audit does.

## Plan the audit

- Scope: which processes, documents and period.
- Participants: the process owners and an independent auditor.
- Method: read the documents, sample records, interview, and compare the two.
- Output: a short report with non-conformities, improvements and owners.

## Questions to ask

| Area | What to check |
|---|---|
| Document currency | Is each procedure current, with an owner, a review date and a version? Do documented processes match what is done? |
| Dormant documents | Are there procedures for systems or services that are no longer used? |
| Risk management | Is the risk procedure actually followed with a recent risk assessment on record? |
| Incident and service desk | Does a documented incident or request process exist that reflects real practice? |
| Access management | Do the new-user and access-request sub-processes link to the forms or records they mention? |
| Asset hand-over | Is there a form for issuing and returning devices, and is it used and stored? |
| Media disposal | Is the disposal procedure current and are records kept? |
| Acceptable use | Is the acceptable-use instruction current and the signed copy retained? |
| Information security policy | Does a policy exist for every company in scope, and is it approved and communicated? |
| Training | Have all staff completed the required training, including environmental and security modules? |
| Formatting and control | Are document headers, codes and revision tables consistent? |

## After the audit

1. Log each non-conformity with an owner, a root cause and a due date.
2. Track corrective actions to closure and check the fix.
3. Turn repeated findings into a process change.
4. Use improvements to simplify, for example one form that covers both hand-over and return, which also reduces paper.

## Measures

- Open findings by age.
- Documents past their review date.
- Repeat findings across audits.

## Why it matters

A clean internal audit shortens an external one and shows that the team controls its own processes.
