# Sanitization policy

Everything in this repository is written so that it can be public without harming an employer, a customer, a supplier or the author.

## What is never included

- Company, group, supplier, vendor, auditor and contract names tied to a real engagement.
- Person names and job titles that identify a person.
- Host names, IP addresses, domain names, port lists and diagrams with real addressing.
- Credentials, keys, tokens, certificates, internal URLs and ticket numbers.
- Amounts, prices, budgets, salaries, sizes and headcounts of any organization.
- Audit findings, weaknesses, incidents or gaps of any organization, and anything covered by an NDA.
- Copies of employer documents, even edited ones. Everything is written fresh.

## What is included

- Methods, decision criteria and reusable structures.
- Generic examples and illustrative figures, labelled as illustrative.
- Checklists and templates that carry no identifying content.

## Checks before every publication

1. Read the text against the lists above.
2. Search the files for names, address patterns and secret patterns.
3. Ask: could a colleague, supplier or auditor recognize themselves or their contract? If yes, generalize further.
4. Describe a plan as a plan and an example as an example.

If you think something here should not be public, open an issue and it will be removed.
