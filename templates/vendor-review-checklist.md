# Vendor review checklist

- [ ] Service scope and exclusions are written down
- [ ] SLA: response and resolution times, and what happens when missed
- [ ] Price model is clear (per unit, tiers, renewal uplift)
- [ ] Contract end date and notice period are in the calendar
- [ ] Exit terms: data return format and timeline
- [ ] Access: named accounts, least privilege, session records
- [ ] Security: how the vendor protects your data, and how incidents are reported
- [ ] Evidence: regular service reports you can check against the contract
- [ ] Review date set, owner named
